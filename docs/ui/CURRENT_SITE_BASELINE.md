# Current Site Baseline

The current public site presents GLAMOURSPHAIR as a luxury hair brand centered on premium wigs, extensions, ready-to-wear units, premium hair care, WhatsApp support, secure online payment, Abuja studio service, and Nigeria/international delivery.

## Current homepage positioning
Observed public messaging includes:
- “Luxury Hair Experience”
- “You deserve Luxury”
- premium wigs and hair extensions
- Paystack checkout
- cards accepted worldwide
- delivery across Nigeria and internationally
- consultation booking

## Current collections and merchandising
Observed:
- The HD Lace Edit
- The Bouncy Collection
- Signature Collection
- Awoof Sales
- Best Sellers / New Arrivals style merchandising

The public catalog currently exposes roughly 26 items across the combined collection view, enough that product discovery deserves deliberate UX.

## Current search
The page currently exposes a search field similar to:
`Search wigs, serum, blonde...`

Audit query matching, no-results recovery, keyboard behavior, mobile behavior, and state stability.

## Current product cards
Cards currently expose combinations of:
- image;
- badge/status;
- current price;
- original price when on sale;
- product name;
- short description;
- collection;
- length;
- weight;
- lace/fitting dimensions;
- Add to Cart;
- WhatsApp.

This is a useful foundation, but some details may be better placed in a proper product-detail view.

## Current direct-buy model
The site supports both Add to Cart and WhatsApp. Preserve the dual pathway, but clarify the role of each.

## Current trust signals
The site currently communicates:
- secure Paystack checkout;
- clear NGN totals;
- local/international delivery;
- delivery fees after location selection;
- live USD/GBP estimates;
- WhatsApp support.

## Current support
Observed:
- custom unit request;
- consultation/help;
- Abuja studio;
- phone/WhatsApp;
- business hours;
- email;
- WhatsApp channel;
- newsletter.

## Audit questions
Verify in the repository:
1. Are all products linked to a proper product-detail experience?
2. Are search/collection states reflected in URL/history where useful?
3. Is sorting available?
4. Are filters needed for price, length, lace/fitting, sale status, availability, texture/color based on real data?
5. Are duplicate-looking product names variants or separate SKUs?
6. Is inventory real?
7. Does Add to Cart preserve exact variant/spec?
8. Does WhatsApp prefill product context?
9. Are delivery fees understandable before payment?
10. Are currency conversions clearly estimates?
11. Are shipping/returns policies easy to access?
12. Is product structured data accurate?
