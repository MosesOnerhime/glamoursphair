# Ecommerce Conversion Standards

## Homepage
The homepage should quickly:
1. establish the luxury brand;
2. route users into relevant collections;
3. show strong product highlights;
4. reduce purchase anxiety.

Do not turn it into an unstructured endless catalog.

## Promotion hierarchy
If multiple promotions exist, keep one primary and make secondary promotions quieter. Remove expired promotions. The “Buy 2 hairs, get free installation” offer must only remain prominent while actually valid.

## Product-card hierarchy
Use:
- image/title -> detail;
- Add to Cart -> purchase;
- WhatsApp -> support/question.

Avoid equal visual weight for every action.

## Cost transparency
Before payment, users should understand item total, shipping/delivery treatment, currency, estimates, and final payable amount.

## Cart
Show exact item, selected specs, quantity, unit price, subtotal, delivery treatment, edit/remove, and checkout.

## Checkout
Keep focused. Avoid forced registration, unrelated navigation, aggressive upsells, or prechecked marketing consent unless the current business flow explicitly requires them.

## WhatsApp
Use for:
- ask about this unit;
- confirm availability;
- request custom version;
- book consultation.

Prefill product context where possible.

## Zero results
Search/filter zero-results must provide recovery: clear reset, related collection, query adjustment, or WhatsApp help.

## Post-payment
Show clear success/failure/cancel/retry states and order/support next steps.
