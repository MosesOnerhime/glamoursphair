# GLAMOURSPHAIR Anti-Patterns

Do not:
- rebrand;
- replace the palette;
- redesign the logo;
- force a black/gold luxury cliché;
- hide prices/shipping to look premium;
- add fake countdowns, low stock, viewer counts, “just purchased” messages, or cart timers;
- invent reviews, testimonials, press logos, customer counts, certifications, or influencer claims;
- add neon/glow/glassmorphism tech aesthetics;
- overload product cards with full descriptions;
- make WhatsApp the only source of product info;
- add aggressive entry/newsletter/discount popups;
- use tiny low-contrast text for “luxury” minimalism;
- add heavy autoplay video or animation dependencies;
- fabricate schema/ratings/shipping;
- clutter checkout with marketing;
- silently merge or rename duplicate-looking products without reviewing SKUs/variants.
