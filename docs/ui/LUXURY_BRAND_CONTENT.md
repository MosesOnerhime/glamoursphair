# Luxury Brand and Content Standards

Preserve the current confident, feminine luxury tone.

Existing brand phrases such as “You deserve Luxury” and “Hair designed to make every entrance memorable” establish personality. Do not rewrite the whole site into neutral marketplace copy.

## Specificity
Editorial brand language and factual product language can coexist:
- brand: memorable/luxury/confident;
- product: 18 inch, 13×4 lace, verified weight, fit, etc.

## Repetition
Audit overuse of words such as luxury, premium, polished, and confidence. Repetition can weaken credibility.

## Collection naming
Preserve official product and collection names. “Awoof Sales” is distinctive; do not automatically replace it with generic “Sale”.

## Abuja + international positioning
Use Abuja studio support as a real trust signal while preserving international-delivery positioning.

## Newsletter
Keep value clear: restocks, new drops, offers, shopping updates. No prechecked consent.

## WhatsApp channel vs direct support
Channel = restocks/offers/new arrivals.
Direct WhatsApp = questions/orders/support/custom requests.

## Social proof
Do not invent reviews, stars, testimonials, press, influencer claims, or customer counts.
