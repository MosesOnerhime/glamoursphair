# Product Discovery Standards

Baymard research shows product-list, filtering, sorting, and search quality strongly affect shoppers' ability to find suitable products.

## Search
Support obvious verified terms such as:
- product name;
- collection;
- length;
- texture/type;
- color;
- lace term.

Provide clear empty/no-result states and stable keyboard focus.

## Filters
Only add filters backed by real product data, potentially:
- collection;
- price;
- length;
- lace/fitting;
- availability;
- sale status;
- color;
- texture/hair type.

## Sorting
Useful options may include:
- Featured;
- Newest;
- Price Low to High;
- Price High to Low.

Only add Best Selling if real sales ranking exists.

## Applied filters
Show active filters, allow removal, and provide Clear all.

## Desktop/mobile
Desktop can update results immediately when practical.
Mobile should use a clear Filter/Sort trigger and drawer/sheet, with generous targets and an explicit apply action if that avoids jarring updates.

## Product list information
Prioritize:
1. image;
2. name;
3. price;
4. sale/original price;
5. 1–3 comparison-critical specs;
6. status;
7. purchase/support action.

## Variants
If multiple cards are effectively one product with different lengths/specs, audit whether they should remain separate SKUs or become variants. Do not merge blindly.

## State continuity
Where practical, preserve search/filter/scroll state when returning from a product page.
