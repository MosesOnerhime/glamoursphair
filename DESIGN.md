# GLAMOURSPHAIR Design Direction

## Brand identity
The site should remain recognizably GLAMOURSPHAIR: luxurious, feminine, polished, premium, warm, confident, editorial, and easy to shop.

Do not turn it into a generic Shopify-looking store, black/gold cliché luxury template, tech site, glassmorphism site, or generic SaaS UI.

## Core principle
**Luxury should come from confidence, restraint, photography, typography, and detail — not from friction.**

A luxury site still needs obvious navigation, clear prices, clear product details, understandable shipping, accessible controls, fast loading, and easy checkout.

## Existing positioning to preserve
The public site currently emphasizes:
- premium wigs and hair extensions;
- luxury ready-to-wear units;
- HD Lace;
- Bouncy Collection;
- Signature Collection;
- Awoof Sales;
- secure Paystack checkout;
- Nigeria/international delivery;
- USD/GBP estimates;
- WhatsApp support;
- Abuja studio;
- consultation/custom orders.

## Visual hierarchy
Shopping priority:
1. product image;
2. product name;
3. price;
4. essential attributes;
5. Add to Cart;
6. support/WhatsApp.

Do not let decorative labels compete with names and prices.

## Typography
Preserve the established type direction. Audit low contrast, tiny metadata, excessive hero scale, awkward mobile wraps, and excessive all-caps.

## Color
Preserve the current palette. Improve only contrast, state clarity, focus, errors, success, and semantic consistency.

## Imagery
Real product imagery is the primary luxury proof. Prioritize front/side/detail views, hairline/lace close-ups, texture/movement, and full-length product views where real assets exist.

Reserve image dimensions, use responsive sizes, lazy-load below-fold images, and do not lazy-load the true LCP image.

## Cards
Keep product cards clean and comparison-friendly. Avoid heavy shadows, nested cards, too many badges, and full descriptions in the grid.

## Badges
Use restrained, real statuses such as existing Best Seller, New, Premium, Trending, Limited, or Exclusive only when meaningful.

## WhatsApp
WhatsApp supports product questions, custom orders, availability confirmation, consultation, and after-order support. It must not replace clear ecommerce product information.

## Motion
Keep motion elegant and light. No long intros, scroll hijacking, delayed price/action visibility, or `transition: all`. Respect reduced motion.

## Mobile
Mobile is a first-class shopping context. Search, products, prices, Add to Cart, cart, and WhatsApp must remain usable without overlap or cramped controls.
